From an extracted evidence package, the offline command is
\begin{quote}\small
\texttt{python -B reproduce\_v4/reproduce.py --out ../v4-recomputed}
\end{quote}
It requires Python, NumPy and Matplotlib, but no simulator or network. It
verifies packaged hashes, recomputes source tables and paired intervals from
individual labels, reconstructs U2 results from sealed choices and future
endpoints, verifies operating-point summaries and the claim ledger, and
regenerates the three main figures. MuJoCo imports and network connections are
blocked during this computation. Three complete primitive-control traces are
also provided for raw rescoring and independent-code reference reconstruction.

This is label-level aggregate reproduction plus three raw examples, not an
independent physical adjudication of every experiment. Most original NPZ traces
remain in the local research archive; a complete hash/size inventory is
included, but no public download location is asserted. This access limitation
must be resolved before claiming fully open raw-evidence reproducibility.
The historical appendix supplies the original V3 bootstrap and library
comparisons without pooling historical and new outcomes.
