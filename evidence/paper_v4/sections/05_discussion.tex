\paragraph{What the labels do and do not establish.}
A contact-record rejection can be conservative for realized no-load without
being wrong for literal no-contact. A loaded state can retain after a specified
removal without proving that the load never mattered earlier. A successful
added continuation does not demonstrate native acquisition, autonomous recovery,
transport, or real-world grasping. Keeping these labels separate avoids an
unjustified progression from a screenshot to a mechanics claim to a policy
capability claim.

\paragraph{Measurement limits.}
The signals are privileged simulator quantities. Raw-evidence reconstruction
uses separate code but the same engine, force solver, recorded transforms and
task specification; it cannot validate the physical model. There is no
independent human annotation, masked external implementer, or hardware test.
Unknown passive paths and conservative mesh bounds may abstain even when a
physical grasp is adequate. Including target free-joint damping in the
non-hand-load budget is a declared modeling choice, not evidence that such
damping corresponds to an unwanted real contact. Fetch specifically exposes
the consequences of that choice.

\paragraph{Intervention and sampling limits.}
Mask removal is an explicit simulator intervention, not a physically smooth
support withdrawal. Development timestep, solver and withdrawal checks bound
some numerical sensitivity but do not make these interventions universally
equivalent. Branches replay fixed command tapes; a real closed-loop controller
could recover or fail differently. The 240 initializations share eight
apparatus groups, Allegro varies six familiar primitive families, and DGB has
only four held-out object identities with possibly curated pose records.
Neither bootstrap intervals nor large candidate counts create additional
independent source diversity. Formal labels must not be recycled as clean test
evidence after future tuning.

\paragraph{Program development and reporting.}
Human--agent heuristic iteration supplies the frozen libraries; no controlled
human-only, agent-only or reinforcement-learning comparison is performed. The
study does not establish an agent contribution to grasp quality. Failed
engineering attempts, unsupported sources, development reuse, and negative
formal outcomes are retained in the artifact ledger. All prior releases remain
unchanged; this report is a new version of the same research program, not an
independent replication of its historical studies.
