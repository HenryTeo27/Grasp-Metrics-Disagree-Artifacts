The bounded V4 research matrix and manuscript are complete. The hoped-for
incremental evaluator/selection advantage is **not supported**. This release is
a narrower measurement/negative-result study, following the plan's stopping
rule rather than adding favorable seeds or a new post-test method.

an independent LaTeX source ZIP, a compact evidence ZIP and machine-readable
verification. Original V3 sources/releases remain protected. Retired earlier
releases remain in the user-authorized F: archive; they were not overwritten.

## Deviations And Limits Kept Open

Five Allegro traces fail the unchanged geometry-consistency assertion at roughly
one nanometer. A separately hashed, post-failure wrapper stores invalid results
without relaxing the assertion, replacing cases or seeing future labels. One
DGB record remains invalid after a reproducible instability. Original files,
native outcomes and denominators are preserved. This is not pre-test recovery
code, independent blinding or a reason to hide invalid evidence.

No B5 improvement over B4 is demonstrated; degenerate zero-discordance intervals
do not prove population equivalence. The no-contact artifact is real on controls
but rescues zero old natural exclusions. Fetch native 100/100 and DGB native
66/100 remain separate from their added-task outcomes. DGB's environmental
withdrawal is a no-op; its four identities do not establish broad transfer.

Native DGB force-test/staging costs were executed but not metered. The budget
ledger names this omission and cannot be used as an exhaustive cost comparison.
No human-blinded annotation, learned-acquisition source, hardware experiment,
second physics engine or controlled causal agent-effect study is completed.

The evidence ZIP contains all individual labels and only three complete raw
control examples; most original NPZs remain local with a hash/size inventory.
There is no public raw-trace URL. Existing isolated-environment checks are not
a clean-machine dependency installation. Third-party assets are excluded;
missing assets are not treated as PASS. The original merged-paper README remains
unchanged; `README_V4.md` is the new entry point. Public hosting, source-code
licensing, external review and venue-specific submission remain author decisions.

Scientific support and engineering completion are deliberately not conflated.
The experiment is finished; a strong positive method claim has not been achieved.
