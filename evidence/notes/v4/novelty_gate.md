# Nearest-Neighbor And Claim Gate

This is a measurement-validity study, not a claim to have invented temporal
monitoring, force closure, counterfactual simulation, or evaluator-based selection.

| Existing capability | Relevant source | Proposed distinction requiring evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporal safety monitoring and validation | SafeManip v3 | Distinguish contact representation, observed load and conditioned dependence; measure both rejection directions |
| Physical grasp stability and multi-hand tests | BODex / DexGraspBench | Audit declared program outcomes and preservation of task semantics; do not substitute a weaker stability benchmark |
| Learned evaluator and grasp selection | Get a Grip | Test one frozen decision route with independent outcomes and equal cost; do not assert novelty of selection itself |
| Wrench tests and downstream use | Grasp to Act | Quantify where historical no-load labels and future-use labels differ, without claiming either is the other's truth |
| Metamorphic physical checks | PHYFU | Test only relations with verified premises in frictional contact dynamics |
| Fine-grained execution evaluation | Eval-Actions v2 | Supply specific physical measurement errors and utility, not another generic beyond-success narrative |

The source links and verified versions are recorded in Section 4 of the
integrated plan. Novelty remains conditional on the controls, natural sources
and decision results. If a well-tuned simple wrench threshold performs equally
well, the correct conclusion is a simpler instrument or a bounded negative
finding, not an unsupported stronger algorithm claim.
