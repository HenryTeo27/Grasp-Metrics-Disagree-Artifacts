# Formal Negative Findings

1. B5 adds no observed accuracy benefit over the information-matched B4 monitor:
   both match 77 positive and 163 negative control references. The paired effect
   is zero. A zero-discordance percentile interval is not population equivalence.
2. B5 adds no U2 selection benefit: B4/B5 choose identically on 120/120 scenes;
   both accept 13 and return 10 usable futures at K6, with 107 abstentions.
3. Replacing no-contact by no-load rescues none of 85 historical excluded scenes.
   The representation artifact in controls does not establish broad natural
   false rejection or a controller improvement.
4. Fetch's 100 native successes do not imply added no-load success: 52 fail its
   common task conditions and 48 remain indeterminate from declared damping
   torque accounting. The native expert must not be called 0%-successful.
5. DGB withdrawal is a no-op in an environment-free model. Its 70 added passes
   provide portability evidence, not an external support-dependence validation.
6. B6 cannot assess the selected external states with unsupported/unbounded
   actuation. Its inapplicability is not converted into a physical failure.
7. Five Allegro traces are invalid under the frozen geometry consistency check;
   one DGB run has invalid telemetry following a reproducible native instability.
   None is replaced, dropped from assigned denominators, or tuned away.
8. The independently learned-acquisition source and human-blinded adjudication
   objectives remain unsupported. Local code independence and timestamps do not
   substitute for either.
9. Full raw evidence is not publicly hosted. The compact archive's offline
   verification is label-level plus three raw controls, not complete physical
   adjudication or clean-machine/full-matrix dynamic reproduction.

Source: `analysis/formal_results.json`, `reports/publication_facts.json`,
`reports/data_quality.json`, `retrospective/final_adjudication/summary.json` and
the unchanged G2 protocol. These results terminate the planned V4 experiment;
they do not automatically authorize a V5 search for favorable significance.
